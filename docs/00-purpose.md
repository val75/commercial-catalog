# Commercial Catalog

## Purpose

Import vendor quotes into a structured database so they can be searched, compared, and later used to build customer quotes.

The system replaces manual spreadsheets used to record vendor quotes and hardware configurations.

The initial focus is on bare metal servers.

Future phases will support pricing, product catalog management, and customer quote generation.

---

## Inputs

- PDF quotes
- Email messages
- DOCX documents
- Excel BOMs
- Copy and paste configurations and quotes from emails and text messages

---

## Outputs

- Structured quote records
- Normalized hardware configurations
- Searchable component database
- Historical vendor pricing

---

## Out of Scope (Phase 1)

- Customer quotes
- Customer portal
- Inventory management
- Ordering
- Billing
- Provisioning
- Asset lifecycle

---

## Success Criteria

A user can:

1. Upload a vendor quote.
2. Review AI-extracted information.
3. Approve the extracted data.
4. Search previously imported hardware.
